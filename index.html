Vision-Based Fire & Smoke Detection with IoT Automation

Problem: traditional fire detection leans on smoke/heat sensors that trigger only after fire has already spread. This system watches for it instead.

A CNN classifies live webcam frames into fire, smoke, or neutral, trained on a 32,400-image dataset (10,800 per class) at 160×160 resolution. A confidence threshold suppresses shaky, low-confidence frames so alerts stay reliable. On a positive detection, the system talks to an ESP32 over MQTT (broker.hivemq.com) to trigger a buzzer, relay, and indicator LEDs, and pushes a live alert through a Telegram bot.

  • 3-class CNN (fire / smoke / neutral), TensorFlow + Keras, OpenCV live inference
  • Confidence thresholding to reduce false alerts
  • ESP32 + MQTT hardware trigger loop (buzzer, relay, exhaust fan demo)
  • Telegram bot for real-time safety notifications
